# Leave policy

Employees get 12 weeks of paid parental leave after the birth or adoption of a child.

Parental leave must be requested 30 days in advance using form HR-2291.

Unpaid leave of up to 4 weeks can be requested using form HR-2219.

# Holiday policy

Full-time employees get 25 days of paid holiday per year. Up to 5 unused days carry over.

# Expenses policy

Travel expenses are reimbursed within 14 days when submitted with receipts using form FIN-104.

Expense claims above 500 euros need a director's approval using form FIN-140.

# Remote work policy

Employees may work from home up to three days per week with their manager's approval.

# Security policy

Laptops must be encrypted and locked when unattended. Report a lost device within 24 hours.

# Sick leave policy

Sick leave is paid for up to 10 days per year. A doctor's note is required after 3 days.

# Onboarding guide

New hires receive a laptop and a security briefing on their first day.

# HR forms

Form HR-2192 is used to change the bank account for salary payments.

Form HR-2912 is used to report a change of home address.

# Finance forms

Form FIN-401 is used to request a company credit card.

# Overtime policy

Overtime must be approved in advance and is paid at 150% of the hourly rate.
