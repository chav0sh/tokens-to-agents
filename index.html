A visual book for back-end developers

Tokens to Agents

You already build APIs, queues and databases. This book adds the model: the patterns every AI product uses, the tools that fill each slot, and how to design a whole system from them.

One app runs through the whole book: an assistant that answers employees' questions from the company's documents. It starts as a single model call. Each chapter adds one piece from the line above, and Part 5 puts the finished system on the table and shows how to design your own.

The three books behind it

Each chapter ends with where to read more. The book restates ideas in its own words and keeps only what helps you build.

  • AIE: AI Engineering, Chip Huyen (O'Reilly). Evaluation, RAG and agents, the architecture.
  • HOLLM: Hands-On Large Language Models, Jay Alammar and Maarten Grootendorst (O'Reilly). Tokens, embeddings, search.
  • PE: Prompt Engineering for LLMs, John Berryman and Albert Ziegler (O'Reilly). Prompts and the application loop.
